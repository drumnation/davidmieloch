# AI-Native Interview Challenge: Senior Frontend Engineer

## Overview

The purpose of this challenge is to evaluate your ability to design a mobile app interface that can integrate with IoT devices and display real-time data from sensors. The challenge is specifically designed for a Senior Frontend Engineer role with a focus on AI-ML.

### Context

You are tasked with designing a mobile app that integrates with various IoT devices in a smart home setting. The app should be capable of receiving and displaying real-time data from different sensors such as temperature sensors, humidity sensors, door sensors, etc.

### Challenge Parameters
- **AI Maturity Level:** Beginner
- **Format:** Pair-Programming
- **Time Limit:** 1-Hour
- **AI Tools Allowed:** No
- **Team AI Fluency:** Expert

## Challenge Description

Please design a prototype for the mobile app using your preferred design tool. The app should include the following features:

1. Dashboard: A main page that displays a summary of the current status of all devices.
2. Device Detail View: A detailed view for each device that shows real-time and historical data.
3. Notifications: The app should provide notifications when certain conditions are met (e.g., temperature exceeds a certain threshold).

## Implementation Requirements

- Use a design tool of your choice (e.g., Figma, Adobe XD)
- Design should be mobile-first and responsive
- Clearly define user flows for navigating through the app
- Design should be clean, intuitive, and user-friendly
- Consider accessibility in your design

## Evaluation Rubric

### Technical Implementation (40%)
- **Excellent (35-40)**: Exceptional design that clearly demonstrates a deep understanding of UI/UX principles. Designs are clean, intuitive, and accessible.
- **Good (25-34)**: Good design with all core features well represented. Some improvements could be made to enhance usability or accessibility.
- **Satisfactory (15-24)**: Satisfactory design that covers the basic requirements. Significant improvements could be made to enhance the user experience.
- **Needs Improvement (0-14)**: Incomplete or poor design. Significant issues with usability and user experience.

### AI Integration & Workflow (30%)
- **Excellent (25-30)**: Demonstrates a strong understanding of how to integrate AI into the design process, even if AI tools are not explicitly used. Has a clear vision for how AI could enhance the app in the future.
- **Good (18-24)**: Understands the role of AI in app design and has some ideas for future integration. Could improve in articulating a clear AI strategy.
- **Satisfactory (10-17)**: Has a basic understanding of AI's role in app design, but lacks a clear vision for future integration.
- **Needs Improvement (0-9)**: Does not demonstrate a clear understanding of how AI can be integrated into app design.

### Communication & Documentation (30%)
- **Excellent (25-30)**: Exceptional communication and documentation. Clearly explains design decisions and how they align with user needs and business goals.
- **Good (18-24)**: Good communication and documentation. Clearly explains most design decisions, but may lack depth in some areas.
- **Satisfactory (10-17)**: Satisfactory communication and documentation. Provides a basic explanation of design decisions, but lacks depth and clarity in some areas.
- **Needs Improvement (0-9)**: Poor communication and documentation. Does not clearly explain design decisions or how they align with user needs and business goals.

## Interviewer Notes

### Key Questions to Ask

1. "Why did you choose this particular layout for the dashboard?"
2. "How did you approach the design of the device detail view?"
3. "Can you explain your thought process for designing the notification system?"
4. "How did you take accessibility into account in your design?"
5. "How would you envision AI being integrated into this app in the future?"

### Red Flags

- Unable to clearly explain design decisions
- Design does not take into account user needs or business goals
- Does not consider accessibility in design
- Does not have a clear vision for future AI integration

### Green Flags

- Clear and thoughtful explanation of design decisions
- Design aligns with user needs and business goals
- Demonstrates a strong understanding of accessibility in design
- Has a clear vision for future AI integration
