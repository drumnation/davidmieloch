# AI-Native Interview Challenge: Tech Lead

## Overview

For this challenge, you are tasked with creating a high-level design of a responsive web application that integrates AI-powered features. This will test your ability to understand project requirements, make architectural decisions, and effectively use AI tools in your workflow.

### Context

You are working on a project to build a responsive web application with modern UI/UX best practices that integrates AI-powered features.

### Challenge Parameters
- **AI Maturity Level:** Intermediate
- **Format:** Take-home Challenge
- **Time Limit:** 4 Hours
- **AI Tools Allowed:** Yes
- **Team AI Fluency:** Familiar

## Challenge Description

Your task is to create a high-level design document for the web application, including:

1. An overview of the proposed architecture, including frontend and backend components, key systems, and data flows.
2. A detailed design of the AI integration, explaining how AI capabilities will be used and how they will interact with other parts of the system.
3. A plan for implementing responsive design and UX best practices.
4. A high-level timeline for the implementation, broken down by key tasks and milestones.
5. Identification of potential risks or technical debt and a strategy for managing these.

## Implementation Requirements

- Design document should be clear, well-structured, and professional in tone.
- Architecture should be appropriate for the project context and requirements.
- AI integration should be thoughtful, leveraging AI as a multiplier rather than a replacement for traditional coding.
- Responsive design and UX practices should align with modern best practices.
- Timeline should be realistic and account for potential delays or risks.
- Risk management strategy should be proactive and sustainable.

## Evaluation Rubric

### Technical Implementation (40%)
- **Excellent (35-40)**: Design is robust, scalable, and well-suited to the project requirements. Considers edge cases and potential pitfalls, with a clear strategy for managing risks and technical debt.
- **Good (25-34)**: Design is sound, with most key elements well thought out. Some areas may lack detail or not fully address potential risks.
- **Satisfactory (15-24)**: Design covers the basic requirements but may lack depth or foresight in some areas.
- **Needs Improvement (0-14)**: Design is incomplete or unsuitable for the project requirements.

### AI Integration & Workflow (30%)
- **Excellent (25-30)**: Sophisticated AI usage that truly enhances the product and workflow. Demonstrates deep understanding of AI capabilities and how to integrate them effectively.
- **Good (18-24)**: Solid AI usage that adds value to the product and workflow. Some areas could be improved for better AI integration.
- **Satisfactory (10-17)**: Basic AI usage that fulfills the requirement but doesn't fully leverage the potential of AI.
- **Needs Improvement (0-9)**: AI usage is minimal, ineffective, or poorly integrated.

### Communication & Documentation (30%)
- **Excellent (25-30)**: Design document is exceptionally clear, well-structured, and professional. Explains reasoning behind design decisions and clearly outlines the implementation plan.
- **Good (18-24)**: Design document is clear and well-structured, with good explanation of design decisions. Some areas could be more detailed or better explained.
- **Satisfactory (10-17)**: Design document covers the basics but lacks depth or clarity in some areas.
- **Needs Improvement (0-9)**: Design document is unclear, incomplete, or unprofessional.

## Interviewer Notes

### Key Questions to Ask

1. "Can you explain your thought process behind the proposed architecture? What were the key considerations?"
2. "How did you decide on the specific AI features to integrate? How do they add value to the product?"
3. "What responsive design and UX practices did you plan to implement and why?"
4. "How did you estimate the timeline for implementation? What factors did you consider?"
5. "Can you discuss your risk management strategy? How do you plan to balance project deadlines with technical debt?"

### Red Flags

- Unable to explain their design decisions or justify their choices
- Treats AI as a replacement for traditional coding rather than a multiplier
- Does not consider responsive design or UX best practices
- Unrealistic timeline or lack of consideration for potential risks
- Does not consider or plan for technical debt

### Green Flags

- Demonstrates deep understanding of architecture design and AI integration
- Thoughtfully considers the user experience and responsive design
- Provides a realistic timeline and accounts for potential risks
- Proactively identifies and plans for technical debt
- Communicates clearly and professionally in their design document
